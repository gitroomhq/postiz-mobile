Postiz is the agentic social media scheduler — run your social media on autopilot with AI agents, and stay in control from your pocket.

Let AI agents plan, draft, and schedule your content to 30+ networks: X (Twitter), Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, Mastodon, Bluesky, Discord, Slack, Telegram, and more. The mobile app is your command center for everything they produce.

YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA ON AUTOPILOT
• AI agents draft and schedule posts for you — review, edit, and approve them on the go
• Works with the AI tools you already use, driving Postiz through its CLI and MCP server
• Automations keep your channels active while you focus on the work that matters

STAY IN CONTROL
• See your entire posting schedule at a glance in the built-in calendar
• Fine-tune any post per channel before it goes out
• Reschedule with a tap when plans change
• Catch per-channel errors before your post goes out

PUBLISH EVERYWHERE
• Cross-post to multiple channels at the same time
• Attach images and videos from your photo library
• Manage all your connected channels in one place
• Changes made on the web or by your agents show up on mobile instantly

Whether you're a creator, a marketer, or a team managing multiple brands, Postiz keeps your social presence consistent — and with agents doing the busywork, your job is just to say yes.
